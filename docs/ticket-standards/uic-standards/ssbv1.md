title: SSBv1
---

Small Structured Barcode v1 (SSBv1) is a fixed-size bit-packed Aztec code. Its specification
can be found in [TAP TSI Annex B.6 - Appendix C](https://www.era.europa.eu/system/files/2022-11/tap-tsi-technical_document_tap_b_6_0.pdf).
